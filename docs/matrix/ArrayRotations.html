ArrayRotations.Rotation4ShearDecomposition

decomposes full 3D rotation from rotation matrix R into one of the possible sequences of 4 consecutive shear transforms; there are at least 6 variants; and the one with the lowest sum of squares for parameters a,b,c,d,e,f,g,h is chosen. Note that there are still invalid cases near 0,90,180 degree rotation.
