DisplayDemo.Tiny

Time the smallest possible frame: one dot changed, put up a hundred times. What is left after the copying is gone -- the lock, whatever the surface carries over for itself, and the wait for a buffer to come free, which on a screen refreshing at 120 Hz is a refresh.
